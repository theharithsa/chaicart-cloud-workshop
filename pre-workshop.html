Three steps to get ready

  1. Join the Discord server. Use the first QR code or invite button below. Sign in to Discord, or create an account if you need one.
  2. Introduce yourself. Share your name and whether you’re a student, captain or facilitator. Add your team if it has already been assigned, and one technology topic you’d like to learn.
  3. Open the workshop event. Use the second QR code or event button and mark your interest to keep the event details handy.
