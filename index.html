Cloud Computing & Business Systems · Two-day workshop

The ChaiCart Cloud Workshop

120 engineering students become founders of 24 startups in four regions. On Day 1 they build a startup in the cloud. On Day 2 they keep it alive through outages, bill shocks and a murder mystery.

Supplies for 120 students

  • 24 chart papers and about 50 markers
  • 12 sticky-note pads for the Gallery Walk
  • About 200 sheets of scrap A4, cut in half for Paper Planes
  • 24 dice for Error Budget Poker
  • 24 envelopes for the murder mystery
  • Two microphones, one handheld for volunteers and pitches
  • A stamp or coloured pens for Follow the Order
  • Scissors or a paper cutter for the card decks
  • Prizes for 9 awards (including 4 Region Champions)
  • 4 region captains and 1 scorekeeper
