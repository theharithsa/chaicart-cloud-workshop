The ChaiCart Cloud Workshop

A two-day workshop for GM University's 2nd, 3rd and 4th year students. Become founders of 24 startups in four regions. On Day 1, build in the cloud. On Day 2, keep your startup alive through outages, bill shocks and a murder mystery.

Paperless workshop setup

Open ChaiCart Live for Google sign-in, team activities, career plans and digital certificates. Captains review their region in the app. Keep reliable Wi-Fi, chargers, projector, two microphones, four captains and a scorekeeper ready. Printable packs are optional reference copies. Captains propose reviews; the facilitator applies credits.
