Keep the roles straight

Five students, one team answer

CEO: pitch. CTO: design. CFO: costs and ledger. SRE: reliability. COO: submits team answers.

Everyone submits their own polls, surveys, networking completion and 90-day plan. Regional captains are assigned staff accounts, not the team’s CEO.

The most important distinction

Three ways an activity finishes

Reviewed

COO submits → captain Approve for facilitator → facilitator Workshop Apply once → ledger updates.

Panel-scored

Facilitator uses the activity’s scoring button. Quizzes: Reveal and score. Mystery, Poker, Bill Shock and Shark vote have their own apply buttons.

Unscored

Discuss or collect the response, then End. Polls, surveys, career plans and Demo orders do not produce workshop credits.

Use ONE scoring path per award. Check the live ledger; correcting scores uses Leaderboard → Undo last change or a reasoned adjustment. The old static quiz/leaderboard pages are reference tools, not a second live ledger.
